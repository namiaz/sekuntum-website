Questions

Good to know.

The things people ask us most. Anything else, just message us.

Ask on WhatsApp
How do I secure my date?
Send us your date and venue and we tell you if it is free. Pay an RM100 deposit and the date is yours. The balance is settled on the event day.
How long does setup take, and what do you need?
We set up about an hour before you start. We need a space of 10 × 10 ft and a power point nearby.
How do guests get their photos?
Strips are printed on the spot, in seconds, and the number of prints is unlimited for the whole event. You also get an online softcopy gallery afterwards.
Can the strip match our theme or company branding?
Yes. Every package includes a custom strip design. We send it to you first and nothing is printed until you say yes.
Where do you travel, and is there a fee?
We cover Penang, Kedah and Perlis. Transport is RM35 flat in Penang. For other areas we quote first, based on the location.
What are photo sleeves?
Sleeves are the holders guests slip their strips into. They are included with the 4 hour (100), 5 hour (100) and Full Day (150) packages. Need more? An extra 100 is RM100.
Can we keep the booth going if the party does?
Yes. You can add an extra hour for RM150, and you can decide on the day if the crowd is still going.
Do you give quotations or invoices?
Yes. For companies, schools and organisers, just ask and we will send a formal quotation or invoice.
What if my plans change?
Message us as early as you can and we will work it out with you.
